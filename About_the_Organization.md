# Fundación Tarahumara José A. Llaguno, A.B.P.

## Who they are

**Fundación Tarahumara José A. Llaguno, A.B.P.** is a Mexican **non-profit organization** dedicated to the integral development of Indigenous communities in the **Sierra Tarahumara**, particularly the Rarámuri people. The Foundation works to address structural poverty through education, nutrition, and community development programs, always with a strong focus on cultural respect and long-term sustainability.

---

## What they do

The Foundation implements social programs aimed at improving living conditions and creating long-term development opportunities in rural Indigenous communities.

### Education
They support children, adolescents, and young adults in **accessing, continuing, and completing** secondary, high school, and higher education. Support includes financial assistance, academic follow-up, and socio-emotional development.

### Nutrition
They operate programs focused on preventing and addressing **child malnutrition**, particularly during early childhood, through food support, nutritional monitoring, and health education.

### Food and water security
The Foundation promotes community-based projects that strengthen **local food production** and improve access to **safe drinking water**, increasing self-sufficiency and resilience.

---

## Who they help

The Foundation primarily works with:

- Rarámuri Indigenous communities in the Sierra Tarahumara.
- Children and youth in vulnerable social conditions.
- Families facing extreme poverty and limited access to basic services such as education, food, and potable water.

---

## Origin and history

The Foundation originates from the social and pastoral work of **Bishop José A. Llaguno**, who dedicated much of his life to supporting Indigenous communities in the Sierra Tarahumara. His vision led to the formal establishment of the Foundation in 1992, with the purpose of organizing resources and efforts to support community development based on dignity, autonomy, and social justice.

---

## Awards and recognition

Throughout its history, Fundación Tarahumara has received multiple recognitions for its social impact, transparency, and institutional best practices, including:

- National Competitiveness Award – Gold Level.
- AIT Certification granted by CEMEFI for transparency and good governance.
- Razón de Ser Award.
- Eugenio Garza Sada Award.
- Additional recognitions for social and community work in Mexico.

---

## Additional activities

Beyond its core programs, the Foundation:

- Organizes fundraising and awareness events, including sports events for social causes.
- Offers volunteer opportunities for individuals interested in contributing to its mission.
- Collaborates with companies, educational institutions, and civil society organizations to expand its social impact.

---

## Approach and values

Fundación Tarahumara is characterized by:

- A participatory, community-centered development approach.
- Respect for Indigenous cultural identity.
- Transparency and accountability in the use of resources.
- Commitment to sustainable, long-term solutions.

---

## Summary

Fundación Tarahumara José A. Llaguno, A.B.P. works to create meaningful development opportunities for Indigenous communities in the Sierra Tarahumara through education, nutrition, and community strengthening, with more than three decades of social impact in Mexico.
