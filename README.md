# Social_Service_Winter_2025

This repository contains code and final outputs developed during my Social Service work with **Fundación Tarahumara José A. Llaguno, A.B.P.**, focused on data validation, cleaning, and process automation. Through this project, I learned to use **pandas from the ground up**, with no prior background, and gained hands-on experience in **process automation using Selenium**, a tool I had not previously worked with. This social service took place from January the 1st of 2025 up to Januray the 31st of 2025. I was awarded by Tecnológico de Monterrey for my work with the Organization with "Alumno destacado de Servicio Social Invierno 2025" (Oustanding Social Service Student of Winter 2025). 

This experience challenged my skill set at the time and reinforced an important lesson: meaningful learning and growth occur outside of one’s comfort zone. Working on tasks for which I initially lacked the necessary knowledge motivated me to learn independently, adapt quickly, and continuously seek more complex and demanding challenges.

## Task Overview

I was tasked with supporting the organization by organizing and improving datasets related to the educational programs and aid provided to their target population. These programs include:

- **Jóvenes con Sentido (JS)** — Upper Secondary Education / High School (Grades 10–12)
- **Vida Universitaria (VU)** — Undergraduate studies / College / University degree programs
- **Capacidades Organizativas (CO)** — High School and University context with Organizational Skills Training

Each program maintained its own dataset, all of which presented significant issues. While the problems varied across programs, several common challenges were identified:

- Missing data
- Unvalidated or unreliable data
- Inconsistent database formatting across different academic years  
  (e.g., CO records from the 2023–2024 school year were structured differently from those of 2020–2021)
- Incorrect or outdated student status  
  (e.g., whether a student had left the program, was still active, or had advanced academically)
- Among many others

---

## Problem Context

These issues largely stemmed from the absence of a solid **data governance strategy and data culture** within the organization. As a result, data integrity, validity, and consistency were frequently compromised. The organization lacked automated or standardized processes to address these challenges and relied almost entirely on manual correction methods.

Given this context, I was granted full freedom to assist in any way possible, using any tools or approaches I deemed appropriate.

---

## Project Approach and Learning Experience

The project evolved through an iterative process of understanding the organization’s needs, listening to their pain points, and proposing solutions. In many cases, I did not initially know how to implement a solution, but I understood that a solution *was possible*. Drawing from prior knowledge, research, and experimentation, I took the initiative to design and implement automated approaches to address these challenges.

This freedom to experiment, explore unknown territory, and continuously iterate is what made me deeply engaged with the project. I spent many nights learning, making mistakes, debugging, and retrying. Through this process, I built a strong foundation in **pandas** for data manipulation and **Selenium** for process automation—skills that I continue to use to this day.

---

## Impact and Purpose

Organizing and correcting these datasets was critical not only for internal efficiency but also for enabling the organization to:

- Generate **reliable impact metrics** required to demonstrate effectiveness to potential donors.
- Support the future development of a **data-driven website** aimed at improving access to information and enhancing the educational experience for program beneficiaries.

This project combined technical growth, real-world problem solving, and meaningful social impact, making it a defining learning experience in my development.
